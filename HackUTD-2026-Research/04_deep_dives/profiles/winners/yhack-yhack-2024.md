### Tidbits
*YHack YHack 2024 · dataset: winners · tier: overall_top3 · evidence A · confidence high*
**Prizes:** 1st Place [1st]

**What it is:** Web/mobile app that turns an uploaded lecture into 5-10 short vertical videos. A pipeline writes a script, generates music, a talking-head presenter, images and subtitles, then stitches them into TikTok-style scrollable clips, optionally translated.
**Problem / user:** University students overwhelmed by long lectures; page cites over 3000 lecture hours and 80% of material lost.

**Features**
- One-click lecture upload to short reels — Audio extracted and chunked; chunks processed in parallel into 5-10 shorts *[shown_working]*
- Scripted narration with AI presenter — Claude script, ElevenLabs voice, D-ID talking head *[claimed]*
- Generated music and images — Suno AI music via reverse-engineered access; DALL-E images matched to content *[claimed]*
- Subtitles from talking-head audio — Whisper transcription with word timestamps *[claimed]*
- Story-based quick review — 1-2 minute summary from a 1-2 hour lecture *[claimed]*
- Language translation of lecture content — Output in a language chosen by viewer *[claimed]*
- TikTok-style scroll feed — Next.js mobile-first auto-playing feed *[shown_working]*

**Flow:** Upload a lecture (audio or video) → Backend chunks audio and generates script and music style in parallel → Talking head, subtitles and images are produced while music renders → Assets are stitched into shorts → Scroll through the reels in the feed

**Prompt:** YHack (Yale) overall places: 1st/2nd/3rd overall prizes are consumer electronics of the winners' choice; the event offers tracks (Sustainability, Education, Entertainment & Media, Healthcare). Judging weights: innovation 25%, impact 25%, technical difficulty 20%, design 20%, presentation 10%.
**Fit (implied):** Education-track style product; README headline says Best Overall Hack; no stated track targeting.  *(targeted: Best Overall Hack, 1st Place)*
**AI:** Orchestrates Claude (script), Suno (music), ElevenLabs (voice), D-ID (presenter), Whisper (transcripts/timestamps) and DALL-E (images) in a threaded/async pipeline.
**Architecture:** Next.js/TypeScript frontend uploads to FastAPI backend; asyncio, aiohttp and threads run chunked AI calls; Supabase stores results; final assembly stitches media.
**Novelty:** Chains six generative services into a single edited short-form video per lecture chunk and optimized generation from 30 minutes to about 4-5 minutes.
**Scope:** medium · working: YouTube demo, 'first successful tidbits' claimed, repo about 2.8k lines in frontend and backend with env example. · polish: Figma-designed UI, detailed README mirroring Devpost; no tests; speedup stated two ways (5 vs 4 minutes).
**Claims:** Video generation cut from 30 minutes to about 5 minutes | Students spend over 3000 lecture hours | 80% of lecture material is lost
**Gaps:** Video not watched; Unclear which YHack edition (2024) prize page applies

### YGroove
*YHack YHack 2024 · dataset: winners · tier: overall_top3 · evidence B · confidence medium*
**Prizes:** 2nd Place [2nd]

**What it is:** Web app that scores how well a user's movement matches an expert reference video. MoveNet pose keypoints from both videos are aligned with dynamic time warping, with a sliding window to find mismatched stretches, shown as a score, graph over time and tips.
**Problem / user:** People exercising, doing physio stretches or dancing at home without access to gyms, studios or rehab centers for expert feedback.

**Features**
- Similarity score versus a reference video — MoveNet keypoints compared with dynamic time warping *[claimed]*
- Upload a video or use live webcam — Uploaded video sampled at 5 fps; live mode follows the reference in real time *[claimed]*
- Expert reference templates — Library of PT, fitness and dance routines *[claimed]*
- Graph of match over time and misalignment detection — Sliding-window analysis highlights where the user deviates *[claimed]*
- Pose keypoints drawn on screen — Rendered to guide alignment *[claimed]*
- Personalized recommendations — Derived from misaligned segments *[claimed]*
- Social challenge links and leaderboard — Stated future work *[planned]*

**Flow:** Choose a reference routine → Upload a video or start the webcam → Pose keypoints are extracted and compared → See score, graph and highlighted mismatches with tips

**Prompt:** YHack (Yale) overall places: 1st/2nd/3rd overall prizes are consumer electronics of the winners' choice; the event offers tracks (Sustainability, Education, Entertainment & Media, Healthcare). Judging weights: innovation 25%, impact 25%, technical difficulty 20%, design 20%, presentation 10%.
**Fit (none):** Health and fitness feedback tool; page names no track or prize.  *(targeted: 2nd Place (overall))*
**AI:** TensorFlow MoveNet pose detection; scoring by dynamic time warping, not a learned model.
**Architecture:** Next.js/React frontend on Vercel; Node.js backend processing frames, pose detection with Promises for parallel videos, DTW comparison.
**Novelty:** Applies DTW to timing-tolerant movement comparison across dance, fitness and rehab in a browser; fixed slow processing by sampling frames instead of real-time playback.
**Scope:** small · working: Built in 24 hours per page; no demo video or repo read; no accuracy numbers. · polish: Clear writeup of challenges; repo clone failed; no video.
**Claims:** Built within 24 hours | Full-scale posture tracking
**Gaps:** Repo clone failed and GitHub not fetchable; README unread; No video
