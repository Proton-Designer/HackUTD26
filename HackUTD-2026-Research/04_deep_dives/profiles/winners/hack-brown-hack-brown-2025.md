### Patmu
*Hack@Brown Hack@Brown 2025 · dataset: winners · tier: track_winner · evidence B · confidence medium*
**Prizes:** Best First-Time Hack

**What it is:** Patmu is a retro arcade-styled web game where users make music by gesturing in front of the camera to 'pat' virtual drums, piano chords and melodies, with recording and saving.
**Problem / user:** Casual users put off by complicated DAW interfaces.

**Features**
- Gesture instruments — MediaPipe and TensorFlow.js detect hands to trigger drums and piano. *[claimed]*
- Tone.js audio — Sound synthesis. *[claimed]*
- Recording — Save performances. *[claimed]*
- Retro UI — Arcade panel look. *[claimed]*
- More instruments — Bass lines, challenges. *[planned]*

**Flow:** Open web app → Allow camera → Pat virtual instruments → Record and save
