### ARcher
*HackTX 2026 · dataset: sponsor · tier: sponsor_winner · evidence A · confidence high*
**Prizes:** [Best Hardware Hack] Brick House (prize: Nvidia 5080 per member; NVIDIA is the prize, not a confirmed sponsor challenge)

**What it is:** An iPhone augmented-reality tower-defense game. A LiDAR scan of the floor becomes a castle and town; the player defends it with a bow, drawn by touch or by a wearable glove with a flex sensor and IMU that connects wirelessly.
**Problem / user:** Players who want tower defense in their physical room with a physical controller; the medieval theme and hardware track shaped the build.

**Features**
- LiDAR-placed AR castle — Scanned room becomes a walkability map; castle and town built around the player, enemies path around furniture. *[claimed]*
- Siege gameplay — Trebuchets lob fireballs, ram and ladder soldiers attack; player shoots them, win by breaking the raid. *[claimed]*
- Bow glove controller — ESP32-S3 with flex sensor and IMU: fist draws, open hand fires; draw power from finger bend. *[shown_working]*
- Wireless UDP link — Glove joins phone hotspot and sends JSON draw, release and heartbeat packets; releases sent three times to avoid loss. *[claimed]*
- Touch fallback and glove takeover — Bow switches to glove on connect, reverts to touch on disconnect. *[claimed]*
- Occlusion, lighting, haptics, synthesized audio — ARKit depth occlusion and light estimation, Core Haptics patterns, generated sound effects. *[claimed]*
- Glove calibration and test page — Firmware stores open and fist positions; board hosts a live sensor page; Python script provisions a board. *[claimed]*

**Flow:** Enable hotspot and local network access if using the glove → Scan the floor until dots appear → Tap to place the keep and the castle builds outward → Trebuchets roll in and the raid starts → Hold to draw by touch or fist, release to fire → Defend until the raid breaks

**Novelty:** Custom wearable controller with a physical draw gesture driving a LiDAR-aware AR game; nearly all models, textures and sounds generated by scripts.
