### Aero
*UXperience UXperience 2025 · dataset: sponsor · tier: sponsor_winner · evidence B · confidence medium*
**Prizes:** CBRE 1st Place [1st]

**What it is:** A Figma concept for Aero, an AI assistant that pulls files, messages, comments, tasks and references from many work tools into one place and summarizes threads into next steps for UX professionals.
**Problem / user:** UX professionals on hybrid or remote teams overwhelmed by scattered tools, context-switching and buried information.

**Features**
- Cross-tool search and surfacing — Finds files, messages, comments, tasks and references across the tool ecosystem in one view *[claimed]*
- AI summaries — Condenses Slack threads, Figma comment chains and meeting notes into next steps *[claimed]*
- Admin automation — Organizes sprint priorities, flags blockers, suggests design resources, gathers related documents *[claimed]*
- User-controlled permissions — Transparency and control over what Aero can access *[claimed]*
- Assistant character and brand — Supportive, non-robotic persona with a brand identity *[shown_working]*

**Flow:** Open Aero in the workspace → Search or ask across connected tools → Receive summaries and suggested next steps → Review automated sprint and blocker highlights

**Prompt:** Design a product centered on creative expression and authenticity in a one-day design sprint, delivering an interactive Figma prototype, 2-minute video, write-up and slides; judged on user research, design process, impact, innovation and UI.
**Fit (implied):** Addresses fragmented workflows and digital fatigue for hybrid UX staff with a prototype built from research; page does not tie it explicitly to the creative-expression brief.  *(targeted: CBRE track (1st place))*
**AI:** none
**Architecture:** No code; Figma design file and clickable prototype plus slides.
**Novelty:** Framed the problem as workflow fragmentation after feedback that burnout is a symptom; includes a branded assistant character.
**Scope:** small · working: Clickable Figma prototype and slide deck; no working software. · polish: Brand identity, user flows, wireframes and persona-driven research listed.
**Gaps:** no walkthrough text; Figma prototype not opened; no video

### Mosaic
*UXperience UXperience 2025 · dataset: sponsor · tier: sponsor_winner · evidence B · confidence medium*
**Prizes:** CBRE 3rd Place [3rd]

**What it is:** A Figma prototype where tasks and habits become tiles that tint as they are completed and gradually reveal a picture, with a team sprint view and a personal wellness view.
**Problem / user:** Hybrid and remote team members for whom progress feels invisible and who feel disconnected or burned out.

**Features**
- Task and habit tiles — Each item is a tile that tints on completion, revealing an image over time *[shown_working]*
- Team Sprint Mosaic — Shared progress view that celebrates movement without singling out individuals *[shown_working]*
- Personal Wellness Mosaic — Daily habit tracking in the same tile metaphor *[shown_working]*
- Interaction details — Hover tips, tile selection, small task panel and an Ask for help path *[shown_working]*
- Accessibility — Color tokens, non-color status patterns and a reduced-motion option *[claimed]*
- AR wall mock — Show mosaic on a wall with mark-done interaction *[planned]*

**Flow:** Open the mosaic view → Select a tile to see its task panel → Mark progress so tiles tint and picture emerges → Switch between team sprint and personal wellness views → Use Ask for help if stuck

**Prompt:** Design a product centered on creative expression and authenticity in a one-day design sprint, delivering an interactive Figma prototype, 2-minute video, write-up and slides; judged on user research, design process, impact, innovation and UI.
**Fit (implied):** A creative visual progress system for teams and personal wellbeing; the page names the CBRE and General tracks but does not tie it to the brief wording.  *(targeted: CBRE track (3rd place), General)*
**AI:** none
**Architecture:** No code; Figma design file and clickable prototype plus slides.
**Novelty:** Replaces lists and bars with a picture-reveal metaphor and cites external burnout statistics as motivation.
**Scope:** small · working: Interactive Figma prototype and design file; no code. · polish: Accessible color tokens, non-color patterns, reduced-motion option listed.
**Claims:** Cites 75% of remote workers feeling disconnected and 42% burnout in 2024 as context (external stats)
**Gaps:** Figma not opened; no video
Sources: https://devpost.com/software/mosaic-q402zy
