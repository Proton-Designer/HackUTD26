## HackGT 11 (Circus of Inventions) sponsor prizes (official wording, paraphrased) - appended by dd_chunk_042
Source: https://hackgt-11-circus-of-invention.devpost.com/
- Best Use of MATLAB (MathWorks): use MATLAB and/or Simulink exclusively or integrated into another tool; prize a branded speaker plus free license.
- SnapAR Skullcandy Grind earbuds: build a Lens on Spectacles showcasing AI on AR glasses; live demo to judges.
- SnapAR Soundview Speaker: Lens on Spectacles demonstrating AR for fitness and health; live demo.
- SnapAR Elexa Power Bank: AR experience showcasing AI on AR glasses; live or video demo.
- Best Domain Name from GoDaddy Registry: register a domain with GoDaddy Registry to enter; prize a Hack from Home Kit.
- Best DEI Hack sponsored by Fidelity: build financial tech that expands access to financial opportunities and resources for all communities.
- Best Use of MongoDB Atlas: build a hack using MongoDB Atlas (cloud database; student credit and free tier).
- Best Use of Terraform (HashiCorp): use Terraform in the project; community edition is free.
