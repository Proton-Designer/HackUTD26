## WEHack 2025 (appended by dd_chunk_008)
Source: https://wehack2025.devpost.com/
- Capital One Challenge "The Best Financial Hack!": open-ended; innovative payment solution, smarter shopping, more accessible financing or financial literacy; optional Nessie mock banking API; 1st place $250 gift card per member.
- CBRE Challenge: simple web/mobile app acting as a "smart detective" that spots hidden investment risks early: gather and analyze multi-source data, predict risks from patterns, turn complex investment data into clear advice. Judged on creativity, accuracy/reliability, ease of use, real-world usefulness, presentation.
