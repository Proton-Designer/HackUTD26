# HackNotts '26 (University of Nottingham, run by HackSoc)
Source: https://hacknotts-26.devpost.com/
## General brief
Open: website, game, hardware or plain .exe. Judging criteria: Creativity, Completeness, Technical Achievement.
## Prizes (page gives mostly names only)
First Place; Second Place; First Time Hacker (>50% first-timers); Best Hardware Hack; Best 2000s-themed Hack; Spirit of the Hack; Hackiest Hack ([Hackathons UK]: "over-the-top over engineered and held together with string"); Machine Learning Prize; Whimsy Prize (no description); Haskell Prize (no description); Telluvian: Best use of Multiple AI Systems in Parallel; Capital One: Make Credit Secure.
