
## HackUPC 2025 sponsor challenges (source: https://hackupc-2025.devpost.com/)
- Seat "Know Your Cupra" (1st/2nd/3rd): help customers learn their vehicle's features between ordering and delivery, ideally by gamifying the user manual in a user-friendly way.
- InditexTech "Visual Search" (1st/2nd/3rd): web or mobile app using Inditex's Visual Search API to identify/locate similar clothing in the Inditex catalog from real-time photos or screenshots; focus is item identification, not catalog browsing.
- Siemens Energy "Data Center Designer" (1st/2nd/3rd): tool for designing made-to-order data centers; users explore equipment/services for networking, cooling, power, processing and storage needs.
- Grafana "Green Grafana" (1st/2nd/3rd): use Grafana to visualize open data on UN SDGs, focus on environmental goals; dashboards, app plugin, or both; suggested sources UN SDG Data, World Bank Open Data, Our World in Data.
- Vueling "Watch out, Blackout!": minimal app for travelers about to fly that shares critical flight info during a blackout over an ad hoc network; prioritize critical info, low battery, bonus for gamification.
- General prizes: 1st iPad, 2nd JBL Charge 5, 3rd 4K mini projector (no track text).

## (dd_chunk_035) HackUPC 2025 sponsor challenges - paraphrased from https://hackupc-2025.devpost.com/
### Siemens Energy - Data Center Designer
Build a designer for a made-to-order data center: clients explore equipment and service options for a planned data center covering internal networking, cooling, power and processing, matched against external networking, storage and processing loads. 1st-3rd places awarded.
### Revolut - AI in Financial Applications
Build an AI-powered solution for the financial world, avoiding "just another finance chatbot". Can be practical (budgeting) or experimental. Judged on innovation, impact, creativity. Must use AI (LLMs or deep learning) and be a working prototype. Revolut provides no APIs; teams use own accounts or local models. Open-sourcing encouraged.
### Skyscanner - The Perfect Reunion
Design a user-friendly travel planner helping a group of friends in different places find a meeting destination, with everyone weighing in. Suggested ranking factors: eco-friendliness, shared interests (art, food, weather, outdoors), events (concerts, matches), cheapest options via flight/train data.
