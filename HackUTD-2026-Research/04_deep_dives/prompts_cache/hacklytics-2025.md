
## Hacklytics 2025 (Jurassic Age) track prizes (paraphrased from official Devpost prize list)
Source: https://hacklytics2025.devpost.com/
- Sports: apply data science/ML to sports analytics (historical data, scorekeeping, player stats).
- Healthcare: analyze large healthcare datasets using health informatics and computational modeling for public-health or biomedical problems.
- Generative AI: explore generative AI, from creative work to AI-powered solutions for complex real-world problems.
- Finance (sponsored by Capital One): creative solution using financial data and technology to improve financial services and simplify user experiences.
- Cybersecurity: data-driven cybersecurity problems - protecting sensitive information, detecting threats, keeping digital systems intact.
- Overall: Best Overall Hack, 2nd Place Hack, 3rd Place Hack, Most Creative Hack. Each track has Best/Second/Third.
