# HackNotts '25 (Oct 25-26, 2025, University of Nottingham, run by HackSoc)
Source: https://hacknotts-25.devpost.com/
## General brief
Open: build whatever you like (website, game, program) as long as it works. 25 hours. Judging criteria: Creativity, Completeness, Technical Achievement.
## Prizes (names only; page gives no descriptions)
First Place; Second Place; First Time Hackers; Best Hardware Hack; Best AI Feature; Spirit of the Hack; Best Fantasy Hack (Theme Prize); Best Fancy Dress; Best use of Haskell; Hackiest Hack (Hackthons UK); Best Use of Agentic AI on Arm (Hackthons UK); Capital One's Prize; Fraser's Group Prize.
