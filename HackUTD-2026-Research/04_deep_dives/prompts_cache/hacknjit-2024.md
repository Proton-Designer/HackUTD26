
## HackNJIT 2024 prizes (official text)
Source: https://hacknjit-2024.devpost.com/
- Theme: Steampunk (Best Themed Hack).
- First/Second/Third Place: "Best in Show", "2nd Place Runner Up", "3rd Place Runner Up" ($1500/$1000/$500 split).
- Best First-Year Hack: best hack by teams of more than 50% freshmen.
- MLH: Best .Tech Domain, Best AI App with Cloudflare, Adobe Express Add-On, Best Use of Terraform, Best Use of Midnight (build a DApp using Midnight). Star Micronics: predictive model on a transaction dataset.
