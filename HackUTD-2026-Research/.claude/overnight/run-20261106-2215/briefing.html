HackUTD 2026 winner research · run run-20261106-2215 · lead + 2 engineers (eng1, eng2), no subagents · finished 2026-11-07 ~00:30 CST

Bottom line. Every stage through Phase 4 is done. Start with outputs/TRACK_DAY_KIT.md when tracks are announced; it links to outputs/findings.md, outputs/archetypes.md and outputs/sponsor_cards.md. All 1,148 projects were tagged blind and validated. Two independent red-team reviews were applied.

What the research says (short)

  • Show working features. HackUTD 2025 winners showed ≥1 feature working 75% vs 42% for strong non-winners; two working flows 23% vs 0%. One flow alone does not separate them.
  • Nothing visible on Devpost separates 1st from 2nd/3rd (127 track pairs). Why is not in the data; rehearse the live demo and Q&A.
  • General track: plain dashboards are 6% of general 1sts but 48% of strong HackUTD non-winners. Common general-1st shapes: multimodal human-need pipeline, agent/dev-tool automation, hardware sense-and-act (mostly from longer events).
  • Problem-style sponsor tracks (banks, insurance, real estate, energy, NMC2): solve the stated problem literally; 22 of 24 HackUTD 2025 company-problem awards used no sponsor product. Tool prizes (NVIDIA, Spacetime, Photon, MLH): the tool must be non-removable (85% of winners).
  • Stacking General + sponsor happens in a minority (10 of 65 general 1sts); at HackUTD 12 of 69 winners won 2+ awards.
  • Cover, images, video: separate you from weak entries, not from strong ones.
