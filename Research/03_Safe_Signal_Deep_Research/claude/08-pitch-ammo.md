# 08. Judging and Pitch Ammo

## TL;DR
1. Open with: Americans reported a record $15.9B lost to fraud in 2025, and texts were the #1 way scammers made contact (FTC). The FBI counted $20.9B more, $7.7B of it from people 60+.
2. Speed hook: about two-thirds of scams are over within one day of first contact (GASA 2025). That's why the agent has to act during the scam, not after it.
3. Story hook: Google's Nov 2025 lawsuit against "Lighthouse," the China-based kit behind the toll and USPS texts. It hit over 1M victims in 121 countries. Second option: T-Mobile's $33M SIM-swap arbitration payout.
4. HackUTD 2025's NVIDIA winners (Toyotron, DataDriver) both used Nemotron as the reasoning core of an agent that **calls real tools and replans**. Both also won a second sponsor prize. Copy that pattern.
5. Scam-protection winners share three traits: they protect older adults, they alert a trusted family member, and they act for the user instead of just showing a warning. Say "we distrust for you" in the demo.

## B. News story hooks (2025-2026)

### 1. Google sues "Lighthouse," the kit behind the toll and USPS texts (best hook)
- **Date/outlet:** Nov 12, 2025, Axios. https://www.axios.com/2025/11/12/google-lighthouse-lawsuit-china-scam-texts (also BleepingComputer: https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/google-sues-to-dismantle-chinese-phishing-platform-behind-us-toll-scams)
- **Summary:** Google filed a civil suit in SDNY against alleged China-based operators of Lighthouse, a phishing-as-a-service kit that sells text-scam templates impersonating 400+ brands, including USPS and toll agencies. Google says the operation reached over 1M victims in 121 countries and spun up about 200,000 fake sites in 20 days. Lighthouse captures keystrokes live and tricks victims into typing in real bank verification codes.
- **Hook:** "That 'unpaid toll' text you got last year came from a subscription kit that produced 200,000 fake sites in 20 days. Our agent catches the text before you tap."

### 2. FBI: AI voice clones of senior US officials (AI-driven "during" hook)
- **Date/outlet:** May 15, 2025, FBI IC3 PSA I-051525-PSA: https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2025/PSA250515 ; coverage in Cybersecurity Dive, May 16, 2025: https://cybersecuritydive.com/news/fbi-us-officials-impersonated-text-ai-voice/748334
- **Summary:** The FBI warned that attackers were texting and leaving AI-generated voice messages that impersonate senior US officials. They build rapport, then push targets to a malicious link to take over accounts and go after their contacts. The FBI's advice is to verify through a separate channel and set a family code word, which a multi-step agent can automate.
- **Hook:** "If scammers can clone a senior official's voice well enough to fool government staff, they can clone your grandson's. Our agent checks independently before you do anything."

### 3. T-Mobile pays $33M after a SIM swap drains $38M in crypto (carrier-level hook)
- **Date/outlet:** Mar 28, 2025, Android Authority: https://androidauthority.com/tmobile-sim-swap-payout-3539652 (also SecurityWeek: https://www.securityweek.com/t-mobile-coughed-up-33-million-in-sim-swap-lawsuit/amp/)
- **Summary:** An arbitration award, made public in 2025, required T-Mobile to pay $33M to a customer whose number was hijacked in a 2020 SIM swap. The attackers used the number to drain over 1,500 BTC, worth about $38M at the time, even though he had an account PIN.
- **Hook:** "One ported number cost a customer $38M and cost T-Mobile $33M. Our agent watches for a SIM change in the 'before' window." Use this carefully in front of T-Mobile judges. Frame it as the problem Safe Signal exists to solve, not as a dig at T-Mobile.

### Takeaways for our pitch
1. **Show the agent taking actions, not just labeling.** Every NVIDIA winner showed Nemotron calling tools (calendar, email, inventory) and replanning. On stage, have ours do the same: check the link reputation, look up the real toll agency or official number, alert family, and freeze or flag the SIM change.
2. **Name the exact Nemotron model and show the reasoning trace.** DataDriver and TrustIssues named their models (nano-9b-v2, 49B) and their pattern (ReAct). Put the step-by-step trace on screen. That's the clearest "beyond a chatbot" proof.
3. **Stack prizes.** Both NVIDIA winners also won a second sponsor prize. Build one demo that fits the T-Mobile and NVIDIA tracks at once (and Auth0 or similar if it's cheap to add).
4. **Before, during, after, with family in the loop.** Scam winners covered the whole timeline and alerted a trusted contact. Use the 64%-within-a-day stat to explain why the "during" step matters most.
5. **Privacy and auditability score points.** Beacon stripped PII before any AI step, and TrustIssues kept an audit log with human approval. Say what stays on-device or redacted, and show the decision log.
