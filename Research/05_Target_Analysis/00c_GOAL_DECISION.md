# Final decision: primary T-Mobile goal for Safe Signal

Locked Nov 7, 2026, 4:45pm CT. Three independent Sonnet reviews, each given only neutral context (`context.md`: the two briefs, T-Mobile's 7 goals with facts, past winners) and none of our earlier scoring. Their answers are in `opinion_1.md` to `opinion_3.md`.

## Result
| | Reviewer 1 | Reviewer 2 | Reviewer 3 | Our earlier analysis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | **6 Customer trust and security** (high) | **6** (high) | **6** (medium-high) | **6** |
| Secondary (pitch only) | 4 Cut care cost (2 as alternate) | 2 Keep accounts | 2 Keep accounts (+1 line on 4) | 2, with 4 as metric |

**Unanimous: Goal 6 is the primary goal.**

## The goal, as we'll use it
- **Design for and get judged on (Goal 6):** customers are protected, and feel protected, before, during and after a scam.
- **Business "why" (Goal 2), 1-2 pitch/Devpost sentences:** protected customers stay; churn is 1.09% and rising after price backlash, and protection is a reason to stay that price cuts aren't.
- **Proof metric (Goal 4), one line:** scams resolved in the app without a care call (supports T-Mobile's digital/AI cost target), plus detection accuracy on our synthetic test set.
- **Not used as framing:** 1 (upsell reads as monetizing fear), 3 (too generic to demo), 5 (no link to scams), 7 (can't build or demo; Nemotron stays the engine, not the story).

## Risk all three reviewers raised
Goal 6 is the obvious choice, so most teams will be here. We stand out through the scored criteria, not a contrarian goal: a sharp audience choice, the during/after phases (where existing tools are weakest), honest confidence, privacy by design, and a demo judges can touch.

## Next step
Score candidate customer groups against this goal to pick the top 5 highest-leverage audiences.
