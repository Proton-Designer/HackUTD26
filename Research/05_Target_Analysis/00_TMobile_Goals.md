## What "highest leverage" should mean for us
A T-Mobile customer group is high leverage if protecting it moves **several of T-Mobile's goals at once** *and* fits the brief (customers feel protected before, during and after a threat). Scoring criteria to use next:

1. **Customer harm:** money lost per victim × how often they're hit (brief: impact)
2. **Vulnerability:** can they spot it, report it, recover (brief: "customers feel protected")
3. **Value to T-Mobile:** ARPA, account size, growth segment (goals 1, 5)
4. **Churn risk after an incident** (goal 2)
5. **Care and support cost** of their incidents (goal 4)
6. **T-Mobile channel control:** does the scam run through calls, texts or the account (where T-Mobile can act)?
7. **Gap in existing tools** (Scam Shield, phone OS, banks)
8. **Demo-ability in 24h** with synthetic data, plus NVIDIA fit (kit `03-user-research`, step 2)

## Open questions for the T-Mobile booth (kit: "what would make this a win for you?")
