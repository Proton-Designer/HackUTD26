# Goal check: which T-Mobile goal does the Safe Signal brief actually reward?

Nov 7, 2026. Brief: `Research/00_HackUTD2026_Challenge_Briefs.md`. Goals: `00_TMobile_Goals.md`.

## The brief, split into testable parts
- **B1** Protect *customers* from scams and fraud in ways *we haven't thought of yet*
- **B2** Any approach: prevention, real-time protection, education, new security features
- **B3** Scam detection is a core piece, **not the whole product**
- **B4** Go beyond classification: **safeguards and customer-facing experiences**
- **B5** Clear next steps, **honest confidence**, **privacy by design**
- **B6** Synthetic data only
- **Judges:** creativity, impact, usability, security and privacy, detection quality
- **Big unlock:** customers **feel protected before, during and after** a threat
- **NVIDIA (second track):** beyond a chatbot, multi-step workflows, tool integration, real-world applicability

## Verdict
- **Lead with goal 6, framed as *customer* trust** (customers feel and are safer before, during and after a scam). Every line of the brief and every judging criterion points here. Two criteria, "security and privacy" and "detection quality", only exist under this goal.
- **Goal 2 (retention) is the business reason, not the headline.** The brief never mentions churn or revenue. Use retention in one pitch sentence ("protected customers stay"), not as the frame.
- **Goal 4 (care cost) is the proof metric**, e.g. "cases resolved in the app without a care call", plus detection accuracy on our synthetic test set (B5 honest confidence).
- **Goal 3 is the T-Mobile angle**: being the carrier that has your back during a scam is how T-Mobile looks good to its judges.
- **Drop 1, 5 and 7 as framing.** Revenue framing reads as profiting from fear. Broadband/financial services and AI-RAN pull away from the brief. Nemotron stays as the agent's engine, not the story.

## Correction to the previous recommendation
Earlier we said "retention, delivered through trust." Checked against the brief, the order should flip: **trust first, retention as the business case, care cost as the metric.** The brief rewards customer protection and experience. It does not reward churn reduction directly.

## What this means for picking the audience
Weight the audience scoring toward: (1) how unprotected the group feels and is before, during and after a scam, (2) how much harm a scam does them, (3) whether T-Mobile's channels (calls, texts, account) are where it happens, and (4) the gap in existing tools. Value to T-Mobile and churn risk become tie-breakers.
