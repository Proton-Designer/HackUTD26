## 0. Why the target segment is older adults (high value + most vulnerable)
- Ages 60+ reported about $3B fraud losses in 2025 (about $600M in 2020), driven by very large single losses. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/10/ftc-issues-annual-report-congress-agencys-actions-protect-older-adults
- IC3: 201,266 complaints from 60+, $7.748B lost, average loss about $38,500, more than 12,000 lost over $100K. https://www.ic3.gov/Outreach/Brochures/elder_fraud_tri-fold.pdf and https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf
- Older adults who lost over $100K to government/business impersonation: $55M (2020) to $445M (2024); scam script is "move your money to protect it". https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/08/ftc-data-show-more-four-fold-increase-reports-impersonation-scammers-stealing-tens-even-hundreds
- Imposter scams $3.5B reported in 2025; costliest start with a fake bank security alert. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/06/ftc-data-show-people-reported-losing-3-point-5-billion-imposter-scams-2025
- Counterpoint for honesty: younger adults report loss MORE often (20-29: 44% of reports vs 24% for 70-79), but 70+ lose far more per incident. https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/csn-annual-data-book-2024.pdf
- Phone calls were only 11% and texts 7% of 2025 loss reports; website/app 31% and social media 28%. So carrier-visible channels are a minority of loss events overall, but calls are the dominant older-adult impersonation channel (FTC older-adult report: most losses and loss reports from frauds initiated on [truncated in excerpt; check the PDF]). https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/social-media-spotlight.pdf and https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2026-older-adults-report-10192026.pdf

## 3. Ranked five biggest unserved gaps (with evidence)

### 1. Live, coached-payment moment for older adults (During)
- The costliest scams begin with a fake bank/government alert and end with the victim moving money themselves. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/06/ftc-data-show-people-reported-losing-3-point-5-billion-imposter-scams-2025 , https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/08/ftc-data-show-more-four-fold-increase-reports-impersonation-scammers-stealing-tens-even-hundreds
- Network filters judge numbers; a spoofed or new number passes. T-Mobile concedes scammers find ways around. https://www.t-mobile.com/customers/scam-shield
- The only live-conversation detectors are Pixel/S26 opt-in (off by default), a Hiya subscription with call forwarding, or user-triggered scanners. https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/15654065?hl=en , https://apps.apple.com/iq/app/hiya-spam-blocker/id986999874
- Google's payment-side hook is limited to screen-share plus participating apps. https://www.android.com/articles/how-android-scam-protection-works
- Calls to apply: warnings with action buttons beat text (prior 05 evidence), but only if rare and specific; do not over-warn. https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/cedex/news/papers/2023-08.aspx

### 2. Nothing after money leaves (After, older adults and everyone)
- Authorized pushes fall outside Reg E; Zelle's imposter coverage is voluntary, criteria unpublished, revocable. https://www.zelle.com/understanding-fraud-scams , https://www.chase.com/personal/personal-account/faqs/send-and-receive-money , https://clearvaluebanking.com/blog/zelle-imposter-scam-reimbursement-vs-regulation-e
- Freeze path is narrow: 200 actionable of 680 FTC referrals, $10.8M frozen. https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2026-older-adults-report-10192026.pdf
- Official recovery pages are checklists the victim runs alone; FTC "does not resolve individual consumer reports". https://www.identitytheft.gov/report
- Recovery scams then re-victimize: some victims reported secondary losses to people offering to recover money for a fee. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2026/04/reported-losses-scams-social-media-eight-times-higher-2020 , https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf
- Speed matters: banks advise reporting within the hour and asking the bank to contact the receiving bank. https://banrox.com/blog/zelle-scam-refund-rules-2026

### 3. Takeover chain and breach aftermath (Before/During/After, SIM-swap and breach victims)
- SIM Protection and Port-Out Protection are customer-enabled toggles; SIM Protection explicitly excludes Apple eSIM transfers. https://www.t-mobile.com/support/plans-features/sim-protection
- Victims report care/store staff who do not know how to respond; carriers decline to document attacks; victims rarely recover losses. https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-397990A1.pdf , https://www.nclc.org/fcc-must-increase-accountability-in-fight-to-end-sim-swap-and-port-out-fraud
- Speed: accounts are drained "within minutes of an attack". https://epic.org/documents/in-re-protecting-consumers-from-sim-swap-and-port-out-fraud-fnprm
- T-Mobile claims a 99%+ drop in successful swaps since 2022 (company claim), which shows controls work when applied. https://www.t-mobile.com/news/un-carrier/advancing-cybersecurity-at-t-mobile
- Ties to the existing threat map Gap 1 (chain interceptor) and Gap 2 (breach concierge). Mock CAMARA signals only.

### 4. Non-English users across all phases
- FTC: detection tools "are often less effective for languages other than English" because training data is thinner. https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/who_is_being_left_behind_-_naag_presidential_summit.final_.public_version.pdf (2022; still the best primary statement found)
- Hiya's screener/fraud detection supports English, French, Spanish and is US/Canada only. https://apps.apple.com/iq/app/hiya-spam-blocker/id986999874
- Google Messages scam detection covers English, Arabic, French, German, Portuguese, Spanish; Pixel call detection launched English-first. https://blog.google/security/staying-one-step-ahead-strengthening-androids-lead-in-scam-protection/?hl=en , https://blog.google/security/new-ai-powered-scam-detection-features
- Apple's call screening speaks the phone's default language and is available in a limited set of languages. https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/screen-and-block-calls-iphe4b3f7823/ios , https://www.macobserver.com/tips/how-to/enable-and-use-call-screening
- Underreporting: reports often come via relatives who translate; scams exploit fear of authorities. https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/criminals-target-ethnic-communities . Consumer Reports: Black and Latino consumers more likely to lose money to digital scams. https://www.consumerreports.org/money/scams-fraud/black-latino-consumers-more-likely-to-lose-money-in-scams-a7652153980/
- Note: this is a differentiator we can show cheaply (Nemotron is multilingual, check before claiming), but I did not find 2026 quantitative evidence of non-English scam losses; treat as qualitative.

### 5. Family/caregiver loop on the phone channel (During)
- Current caregiver tools watch money after it moves and are paid or bank-gated. https://www.eversafe.com/pricing.html , https://partner.getcarefull.com/tcb/how-it-works
- Bank trusted-contact alerts are per institution and cannot touch the money. https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/protect-older-parents-from-scams
- Pixel's detector leaves the decision to the person on the call ("Not a scam" dismisses it). https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/15654065?hl=en
- Family safe-word advice is the main carrier-side tip; no feature supports it. https://www.t-mobile.com/dialed-in/tips-tricks/avoid-phone-scams
- Honest caveat: consent and privacy design are central; do not monitor without opt-in from the older adult.

Honorable mention: young adults. Largest share of loss events starts off-network (social, marketplace, job, rental), so a carrier layer cannot see it; a "paste/forward to the agent" flow is the only reach. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2026/04/reported-losses-scams-social-media-eight-times-higher-2020

## 4. Implication for target choice
