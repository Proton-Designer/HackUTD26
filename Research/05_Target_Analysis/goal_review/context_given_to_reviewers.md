# Neutral context: choosing the primary T-Mobile goal for a HackUTD 2026 project

## The event
HackUTD 2026, UT Dallas, Nov 7-8, 24 hours. A 4-person student team. Each team can enter up to 2 sponsor tracks. Judging: a short table demo (about 4 minutes per judge visit), Devpost submission with video.

## T-Mobile challenge (verbatim from slide)
**Safe Signal: Protect customers from scams and fraud in ways we haven't thought of yet.**
- You build: Any approach: prevention, real-time protection, education, new security features. Scam detection is a core piece, not the whole product. Go beyond classification: safeguards and customer-facing experiences. Clear next steps, honest confidence, privacy by design.
- You create the data: Build your own synthetic scam messages and scenarios. No real customer data.
- Judges look for: Creativity, impact, usability, security and privacy, detection quality.
- The big unlock: Customers feel protected before, during and after a threat.

## NVIDIA challenge (verbatim), the team may also enter it
Use Nemotron Beyond a Chatbot. In addition to using Nemotron, the project must include: Beyond a Chatbot (not just responding directly to prompts); Multi-step workflows (agents that plan and execute complex tasks); Tool integration (using external APIs and services intelligently); Real-world applicability (solving actual problems).

## T-Mobile's business goals (from its 2026 guidance, Nov 2026 security report, and analyst research)
1. **Grow revenue per account.** "More-for-more" pricing; postpaid ARPA growth target +2.5-3.0% in 2026; service revenue ~$77B (2026), $80.5-81.5B (2027).
2. **Win and keep accounts.** 900K-1.0M postpaid net account adds expected in 2026. Postpaid account churn 1.09% in Q3 2026, rising. Price increases on legacy plans in 2025-26 caused customer backlash.
3. **Differentiate: "Best Network, Best Value, Best Experiences."** NPS reported 20%+ ahead of closest competitor.
4. **Cut cost through digital and AI.** ~$3B incremental Core Adjusted EBITDA from digitalization and AI by 2027; calls to care down 50%+ since 2021; T-Life app ~24M monthly active users.
5. **Grow new businesses.** 5G home broadband to 15M customers by 2030; fiber 3-4M; advertising, financial services, edge/physical AI.
6. **Customer trust and security.** Successful SIM swaps down >99% since 2022; nearly 50% more scam/spam messages in 2025 than 2024; ~3B scam texts blocked in 2025; automatic scam protection for all customers (Scam Shield); preparing for AI-enabled fraud. Past breaches (2021: 76.6M people; 2023: ~37M accounts) led to a $350M settlement and a $31.5M FCC consent decree.
7. **Turn the network into an AI platform.** AI-RAN with NVIDIA and Nokia (first live commercial AI-RAN call Oct 2026).

## Background facts
- Scam losses (US): FTC 2025 reported losses $15.9B; people 60+ reported $3B+. FBI IC3 2025: $20.9B total, $7.7B from people 60+. Phone calls and texts are major contact channels.
- Past T-Mobile hackathon winners: at HackUTD 2025 the T-Mobile track (53 entries) was won by three internal dashboards for T-Mobile product managers built around a "Customer Happiness Index". At DubHacks '26 (Oct 2026) T-Mobile's winners were a secure form auto-filler using T-Mobile synthetic customer data (1st), a hiking satellite-coverage planner built around T-Mobile's T-Satellite add-on (2nd), and a breadboard anti-scam device for older adults (3rd).
- Sponsor judges are T-Mobile employees. Judges score what they see in the demo.
