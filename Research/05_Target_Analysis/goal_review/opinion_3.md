# Opinion 3: Primary T-Mobile goal for Safe Signal

**1. Primary: Goal 6, Customer trust and security.**
Reasons:
- The brief's challenge text is "Protect customers from scams and fraud." Goal 6 is the only goal whose own line names the same problem: "nearly 50% more scam/spam messages in 2025," "~3B scam texts blocked," Scam Shield, "preparing for AI-enabled fraud." Judges can see the link between the demo and the goal without being told.
- Judging criteria "security and privacy" and "impact" map directly onto it. The brief's "privacy by design" and "no real customer data" are trust behaviors. The 2021 and 2023 breaches, the $350M settlement and the $31.5M FCC decree give the pitch a concrete reason to say why trust matters to T-Mobile.
- "The big unlock: Customers feel protected before, during and after a threat" is the trust goal stated as a user experience. A before/during/after demo is scored against it with no translation step.

**2. Secondary: Goal 2, Win and keep accounts.**
Use it only in the pitch's business-rationale slide and Devpost "why T-Mobile" paragraph: the quoted line is "postpaid account churn 1.09% in Q3 2026, rising" plus the legacy-plan backlash. Argue that protection is a retention reason that price cuts are not. Do not build features or metrics around churn. A one-sentence mention of Goal 4 (fewer care calls) is optional.

**3. Avoid as framing.**
- Goal 1 (revenue per account): upsell framing reads as monetizing fear, and contradicts "automatic scam protection for all customers."
- Goal 3 (differentiation): too vague to demo or measure.
- Goal 4 (cost cut): judges score a customer experience, and cost savings are invisible in a 4-minute demo.
- Goal 5 (new businesses): scam protection does not map onto home broadband or fintech.
- Goal 7 (AI platform): AI-RAN is network infrastructure, which a student team cannot show and which the brief does not ask for. Keep NVIDIA/Nemotron as a tool choice, not a framing.

**4. Strongest argument against.**
Goal 6 is the obvious choice, so everyone will pick it and the track becomes crowded. The past winners (HackUTD 2025's internal product-manager dashboards, DubHacks' form auto-filler) suggest judges reward a clear internal business tie-in, which would favor Goal 4 or Goal 2. I still hold Goal 6 because the Safe Signal brief sets the goal itself. Other framings add distance from the prompt. The way to stand out is creativity and execution (before/during/after, honest confidence, synthetic data) rather than a contrarian goal. The DubHacks third-place anti-scam device also shows scam work places well. The risk is real, so Goal 2 carries the business argument.

**5. Confidence: medium-high.**
