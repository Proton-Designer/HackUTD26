# Opinion 2: Primary T-Mobile goal for Safe Signal

**1. Primary: Goal 6, Customer trust and security.**
- The brief's headline is "Protect customers from scams and fraud." Goal 6 is the only goal that names this problem: ~50% more scam/spam messages in 2025, ~3B scam texts blocked, Scam Shield, "preparing for AI-enabled fraud." A pitch anchored here needs no translation for judges.
- The brief's "big unlock" is "Customers feel protected before, during and after a threat." That is a trust outcome. The judging criteria "impact" and "security and privacy" also map directly to trust.
- "Privacy by design" and "honest confidence" in the brief echo the breach history in Goal 6 (76.6M and ~37M affected, $350M settlement, $31.5M FCC decree). T-Mobile judges care about that, and no other goal lets us address it.

**2. Secondary: Goal 2, Win and keep accounts.** Use it in one or two pitch/Devpost sentences only: trust drives retention (churn 1.09% and rising, backlash over price increases). Frame the product as a reason to stay. Do not design features or metrics around churn.

**3. Avoid as framing:**
- Goal 4 (cut cost via AI): it turns the project into an internal dashboard, which is tempting given the HackUTD 2025 winners, but it conflicts with "customer-facing experiences" and "customers feel protected."
- Goal 1 (revenue per account): upselling a security add-on contradicts a "protect customers" message.
- Goal 3 (differentiation): too generic to be tested in a demo.
- Goal 5 (new businesses): no link to scams.
- Goal 7 (AI-RAN): it is network infrastructure and a stretch for a student project, though it could loosely suit the NVIDIA track.

**4. Strongest argument against.** Goal 6 is the obvious choice: Scam Shield already exists, so judges may see "more protection" as incremental. Past winners were framed around business metrics and internal-facing tools, and a goal-4 or goal-2 anchor may look more commercial. I still hold Goal 6 because the track text itself is the Goal 6 problem. The risk is differentiation, which we handle through creativity in the experience (before/during/after) and not by changing the goal. The DubHacks '26 3rd place (anti-scam device for older adults) shows the topic scores well. The 2025 winners answered a different brief.

**5. Confidence: High.**
